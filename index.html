How it works

From first dish to live menu, without the fuss.

No designer handoffs. No clumsy PDF uploads. Just a clear five-step path to a menu customers enjoy using.

01

Create your restaurant

Add your name, story, logo, contact details, and the essentials that make your restaurant recognizable.

02

Build your menu

Organize starters, mains, drinks, and specials into clear sections with descriptions, prices, and images.

03

Choose your look

04

Publish your menu

Preview everything on mobile, publish when it feels right, and keep every future update instantly in sync.

05
