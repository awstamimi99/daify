The short version

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand — in aggregate — how menus are viewed. We don't use cookies to sell your data, and you can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time.

01What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember information about your visit, like your language preference or whether you're signed in. We also use similar technologies — such as local storage — that behave the same way for the purposes of this policy.

02How we use cookies

DAIFY uses cookies across three surfaces: the marketing site (daify.net), the restaurant dashboard, and published guest-facing menu pages. Across all three, cookies help us keep sessions signed in, remember display preferences (like language or template view), and understand aggregate traffic patterns so we can improve the Service and the analytics we show Restaurants.

03Types of cookies we use

Category Purpose Duration Can be disabled?
Essential Keep you signed in, remember items in progress, and protect against cross-site request forgery. Session, up to 30 days No — required for the Service to function
Functional Remember preferences such as language, currency, or menu template view. Up to 1 year Yes, via browser settings
Analytics Understand aggregate traffic and menu-viewing patterns to power dashboard analytics and improve the Service. Up to 2 years Yes, via browser settings
Marketing Measure the performance of our own marketing campaigns on daify.net. Not used on guest-facing menu pages. Up to 90 days Yes, via browser settings

04Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by service providers who help us operate the Service — for example, analytics and payment providers. These third parties may use cookies according to their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review. We only work with providers who process this data as our service provider, not for their own independent purposes.

05Managing your preferences

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Because essential cookies are required to sign in and use the dashboard, disabling all cookies will limit what you can do on DAIFY. You can typically manage cookies from your browser's preferences:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

06"Do Not Track" signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no common industry standard for how sites should respond to it yet, so DAIFY does not currently change its behavior based on this signal — but we continue to limit non-essential cookies as described in this policy.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the Service or the cookies we use change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version, and material changes will be communicated the same way as changes to our Privacy Policy.

08Contact us

Questions about our use of cookies? Reach us at privacy@daify.net or through our contact page.
