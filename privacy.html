The short version

DAIFY collects the account, menu, and usage information needed to run your digital menu and help guests browse it. We never sell personal data, we share it only with the service providers who help us operate DAIFY, and you can access, export, or delete your information at any time.

01Overview

DAIFY ("DAIFY", "we", "us", or "our") provides a platform that lets restaurants, cafés, and hospitality businesses ("Restaurants", "you") design, publish, and manage digital menus that guests view on their own devices. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use daify.net, the DAIFY dashboard, and any published menu pages (together, the "Service").

This policy applies to Restaurant account holders and team members, and to guests who view a published DAIFY menu. Where the two are handled differently, we say so explicitly.

02Information we collect

Account & team information

When you create a DAIFY account, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), business name, and role within your organization.

Restaurant & menu content

We store the content you add to build your menu: dish names, descriptions, prices, categories, photos, branding assets, languages, and template or design choices. This content is yours — see our Terms of Service for how it's licensed to us.

Billing information

If you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment processor collects and stores your card or payment details directly. DAIFY receives only a billing summary — plan, amount, and transaction status — never your full card number.

Guest & usage data

When a guest views a published menu, we automatically collect limited technical data: approximate location (city-level, from IP address), device and browser type, referring page, pages or menu sections viewed, and timestamps. We use this in aggregate to power the analytics dashboard Restaurants see — it is not used to build a profile of an individual guest.

Support & correspondence

If you contact us through the site, email, or in-app support, we keep a record of that conversation so we can respond and improve the Service.

03How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and Restaurant workspace.
  • To render, publish, and serve your menu pages to guests reliably and quickly.
  • To generate the analytics and reporting features inside your dashboard.
  • To process subscriptions, invoices, and payments.
  • To send service communications — account, billing, and security notices — and, if you opt in, product updates.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • To improve the Service through aggregated, de-identified usage trends.

04How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. Vendors who host our infrastructure, process payments, send email, or provide analytics on our behalf, bound by contracts that limit their use of your data to providing that service.
  • Your published menu. Content you choose to publish is, by design, visible to anyone who visits your menu link or scans your QR code.
  • Legal requirements. When required to comply with a law, regulation, court order, or valid legal request.
  • Business transfers. If DAIFY is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a comparable one.
  • With your direction. Any other sharing we'll ask for your consent first.

05Cookies & tracking

DAIFY and its analytics providers use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how menus are viewed. For a full breakdown of what we use and how to control it, see our Cookie Policy.

06Data retention

We keep account and menu data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymize personal data within 90 days, except where we must retain it longer for legal, tax, or security reasons. Aggregated analytics that no longer identify a Restaurant or guest may be retained indefinitely to improve the Service.

07Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Restaurant account holders can access and edit most information directly from the dashboard's Settings page. For anything else — including a full data export or account deletion — email privacy@daify.net and we'll respond within 30 days.

You can opt out of non-essential marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every message.

08Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review of our systems — to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable any available account protections.

09International transfers

DAIFY is based in Kuwait and works with Restaurants and infrastructure providers worldwide. Your information may be processed in a country other than your own. Where this involves a transfer of personal data across borders, we take steps to ensure it receives an equivalent standard of protection.

10Children's privacy

The Service is intended for restaurant businesses and their adult staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service evolves. If we make material changes, we'll notify Restaurant account holders by email or an in-app notice before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

12Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach us at privacy@daify.net or through our contact page.

This document is provided as part of the DAIFY front-end prototype and is intended as a starting point for legal review, not as legal advice.
